Features in detail

From the letterbox to the reply — one tool

Every recording below comes straight from the running application.

01 — Understand

One tap. Understood.

Diwan connects to the e-mail mailboxes you already have. When an official message arrives, one tap is enough — Diwan explains it in your language:

  • What is demanded — as a clear list
  • Which documents are needed
  • By when? The deadline comes first

02 — Write

Notes in, correct German out

You write your reply as a simple note — in German, Arabic, English, Russian, Ukrainian or any other language. Diwan turns it into a correct German letter in the right form. Your own text can be restored with one click at any time. Nothing is sent automatically — you decide.

03 — Ask instead of guess

The AI does not guess — it asks

If information is missing, Diwan asks targeted questions first — "Do you already have the proof of residence?" — and only writes after your answer. The letters are realistic rather than speculative, and the confirmed facts are stated with authority.

04 — Paper post

Real letters, fully digital

Many offices still write on paper — Diwan closes exactly that gap. Photograph the letter; Diwan reads and explains it and recognises deadlines and required documents. The reply goes out as a real letter straight from the app — no printer, no trip to the post office.

05 — Print, post & proof

Printed, enveloped, delivered

When you send, Diwan takes care of everything: printing, enveloping, delivery — by registered mail if you wish. Attachments are printed and sent in the same letter. The proof of dispatch stays in the app, available at any time.

06 — Tasks & deadlines

Deadlines become tasks — automatically

Letters and e-mails turn into tasks, appointments and reminders — with a deadline per required document and a reminder before every appointment. Nothing gets lost by accident any more.

Security & control

Your data. Your control.

🇪🇺

Stored in Germany

Database, documents, background service and AI processing run in Frankfurt am Main — in the EU by default. Which provider processes what is stated openly in the privacy policy.

🔒

No training on your data

Your post is not used to train AI models. That is recorded contractually with every AI provider we use and can be read in the data processing agreement.

👤

The human decides

Diwan never sends automatically. Every letter is reviewed by you and explicitly approved. AI suggestions remain suggestions.

🔌

AI can be switched off

The AI functions can be switched off in the settings at any time. Then no content leaves your mailbox — and the rest of Diwan stays fully usable.
